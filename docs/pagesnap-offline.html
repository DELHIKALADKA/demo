PageSnap

Turn a video of notes into a PDF — one screenshot per page, nothing else. Runs entirely in your browser.

  1. Choose a video from this device (or paste a link to see what can be done with it)
  2. PageSnap finds the pages
  3. Check the pictures, tick the ones you want, reorder or remove them
  4. Optionally capture a frame from a video, or swap one picture for it
  5. Pick the paper, then download the PDF
